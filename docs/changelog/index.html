    Release 1, in development#

    Sprint 11: the live check fixes (20 October 2026)#

    • Closed when. Each Tracked object says when its records count as closed: the status field holds one of the closed values, or one checkbox on the record, such as IsClosed on Opportunity, is checked. The object screen lists every checkbox on the object as "Label (API name)", except Deleted. The choice is saved, exported, and imported with the object's other settings. See When a record closes.
    • Record Closed. While a record is closed, its Intervals carry the new Record Closed flag, and so does every Interval that opens before it reopens. The owner's stay ends at the close, and no Owner Interval opens while the record stays closed. A reopen starts a new owner stay for the owner the record has then. Capture, History and Seed backfill, Catch up, Reconcile, Live values, and the Case metrics all follow the same rule, and the nightly Reconcile puts the flag in step on the open Intervals of the records it checks.
    • Warning. An Interval whose business minutes reach its goal's warn percent is flagged Warning, with Warned At set to the moment it got there. Live values flags open Intervals, and a stay is flagged when it closes. A breached Interval is always a warning too. The Goals screen says what Warn at does, and the timeline names a warning in a stay's details. See Set goals.
    • Breach and warning times to the second. Breached At and Warned At count the goal from the second an Interval entered its value, not from the start of that minute, so under Business hours that close they are no longer up to a minute early. A goal that ran out at closing time had been stamped at the close; it now lands when the hours open again, at the seconds it entered with.
    • Value group and summary columns. Intervals gain Value Group, and Interval Summaries gain Value Group, Record Type, and Snapshot 1, each part of a summary row's key from the first version, and the report types show them. They stay blank for now; value groups come in sprint 13.
    • Links into Sojourn. Each Sojourn Settings screen, Tracked object, and card, and each Health Monitor tab and error status, has its own address. The address follows you as you move, so you can copy it at any time, and a link to an object that is not tracked opens Tracked objects and says so. See Links into Sojourn.
    • Reports and dashboards. The Lead and Opportunity reports average finished stays only, and the change counts per Case leave out Cases never tracked. Reports of open stays leave out closed records by Record Closed, and Longest Open Intervals lists the open stays entered longest ago. The weekly trend runs in date order. SLA Health counts breaches and open Cases and shows the hours to first close, and the dashboards chart counts, not sums, and hours, not minutes. The Case, Lead, and Opportunity report types list only records with Intervals, and the two by-Case reports open on Cases created in the last 30 days. Report types on Intervals show Record Closed, Warning, Warned At, and Value Group.
    • Weekly trends without closed time. Summary rows keep time spent while a record was closed apart, in a new Record Closed column of their own, and Average Hours per Status by Week leaves it out, so no Closed line runs through the weekly charts.
    • Owner changes per Case. A Case closed and reopened by the same owner no longer counts an owner change; only a different owner is a handoff.
    • Longest Open Intervals. A Days Open column gives every stay its calendar days to now, beside the business minutes Live values keeps for recent stays.
    • Imported records. A Case, Lead, or Opportunity imported with its original Created Date starts its first Intervals at that date, not at the import.
    • Deleted records. Reconcile flags every Interval of a deleted record, not only the ones still open, so reports that leave out orphaned Intervals leave out the whole record.
    • Undeleted records. A record brought back from the Recycle Bin is tracked again: its Intervals lose the orphaned flag and carry on from where they stopped. Only the Intervals Reconcile ended at the deletion open again, marked by a new Closed at Deletion checkbox; an owner stay that ended when the record closed, or agent work that had ended, stays ended. Summaries leave out an Interval Reconcile ended at the deletion for the 15 days the record can be brought back and one day more, so the next Reconcile can open it again: a reopened Interval is counted once, when it next closes, and one whose record stays deleted is counted after the 16 days. Reconcile finds records back from the Recycle Bin however many deleted records are still in it.
    • Repair of owner changes on closed records. Create interval resolves an owner change on a record that is closed, with the reason: owner time is not counted while a record is closed, so there is no Interval to create.
    • Rule owners on records created closed. A Case or Lead created closed and assigned by a rule in the same save takes the rule's owner on its first Intervals.
    • Field API names. The object screen shows each field's API name under its label, and the field lists read "Label (API name)", so two fields with the same label are told apart. On a window narrower than 1360 px, the Tracked fields list sits above the chosen fields instead of pushing the page sideways.
    • Intervals tab. Three list views, All Intervals, Open Intervals, and Breached Intervals, and an Interval page in sections (the value, the time, the goal, Live values, the record's snapshot) with read-only fields and highlights at the top.
    • Notifications. They sit over the Settings content, centred on it, just below the Salesforce header whatever its height. Pause, Resume, Turn on, Run backfill, Save settings, and the Overview's job buttons now say what they did, and Save settings keeps your choice on screen while the Overview reloads.
    • Loading and busy. The Tracked objects list loads under its real column labels, and Re-check Flows shows it is at work.
    • Retention. Neither choice is selected until you save one, and the org time zone is named once.
    • Repair. Create interval refuses, with the reason, an error that has no Interval to create; the nightly retries count it and stop after three, instead of looping.
    • Jobs card. Live values says how far back it keeps Intervals current (90 days unless changed), and each job's schedule is read from the job itself.
    • Warnings follow the Interval. Each new Interval carries its warn threshold as Warn Minutes beside Goal Minutes, so changing or turning off a goal no longer changes the warnings of Intervals already started, just as it leaves their Breach. Purge CSV files gain Warning and Warned at columns at the end, the archive keeps each Interval's warning, and summary rows count warnings in Warning Count.
    • Settings files. A file of more than 1,000,000 characters is refused before it is read, and the import box can be read again after an edit. A file with a misspelt setting is refused with Sojourn's own sentence alone.
    • Purge files and the archive keep what summaries group by. Purge CSV files gain Record Closed and Value Group columns after Warned at, and the archive keeps each Interval's Record Closed Flag and Value Group. See Keep or purge Intervals.
    • Open Intervals list view. It leaves out the Intervals of closed records and shows Calendar Minutes To Date, which is always current, in place of Exited At and Elapsed Business Hours, which stay empty until a stay ends.
    • Average Hours per Owner, 30 Days. It leaves out time spent while the Case was closed, as Average Hours per Status, 90 Days does. Share of Time per Status Group says in its description that its groups hold example statuses to edit.
    • Goals. Warn At Percent is refused outside 1 to 100 on every save, not only on the Goals screen and in a settings file.
    • Summary rows. A value, group, record type, or snapshot that contains a pipe no longer shares a summary row with another. Rows built before this change are recounted by Rebuild summaries.
    • Error messages. A failure Sojourn did not describe itself reaches Sojourn Settings, the Health Monitor, and the timeline as a fixed sentence that says what to do, never Salesforce's own words, which can name objects and fields. A save Salesforce refused still shows the reason it gave. Backfill, Catch up, Purge, and the nightly Summaries run report a failed part the same way, in their results and in the Error Log message, and so does a Flow check that could not read your Flows; Salesforce's own words stay in the error's technical details, which only Sojourn admins see. A Live values, Reconcile, or Summaries run with failed chunks says how many on the Jobs card and the Health Monitor, and Salesforce's reason stays in Setup, under Apex Jobs.
    • Read failures. When Reconcile's Recycle Bin check or the Error Log's record names cannot be read for a reason other than missing access, the failure is written to the Error Log instead of passing as no data. On the timeline, such a failure shows as an error instead of an empty history; a reader without access still sees what they may read.
    • Name field and snapshot fields. The object screen lists only fields you can read. The guide says which record values everyone with Sojourn User can read on Intervals, so you can choose a Name field to suit; see Add an object.

    Sprint 10: the last fixes before promotion (20 October 2026)#

    • Loading states. Every Sojourn Settings and Health Monitor screen shows a loading state shaped like its content until its data arrives, instead of an empty page.
    • Busy buttons. The button you press shows the Lanes mark at work until its action ends.
    • Notifications. What an action did appears at the top of the page as a slim Harbor bar, one stack each for Done, Started, and Failed, with the newest in front and a count that opens the list. Done and Started close after 6 seconds; point at one to read the whole message and keep it open. A failure says what went wrong and stays until you dismiss it. Repair results that list problems still show on their screen.
    • Partial saves. When a save of many records partly fails and Salesforce saves the rest again, each saved record keeps an Interval for every value it passed through in that save, including changes your own automation made in the same save.
    • Backfill. A backfill that cannot start, for example because its object is no longer tracked, shows Failed with the reason at once instead of staying Queued.

    Sprint 9 (in progress): review fixes and lock-in (20 October 2026)#

    What is merged so far; promoting the 1.0.0 packages and submitting them for the security review come after sprint 10.

    • Assignment rules. A Case or Lead that an assignment rule routes when it is created starts with the rule's owner, so its first queue time is right.
    • Breach at close. An Interval that closes past its goal is flagged as a Breach when it closes, not only by Live values. A new Breach alerts card on the Goals screen chooses whether owners are also emailed when a stay closes past its goal; it is off by default.
    • Correct Intervals in edge cases. A save that partly fails records every row that saves, Reconcile and Catch up open no blank or unchanged Intervals, and a record with two open Intervals for one field heals on its next change.
    • Health Monitor. Repair recomputes the Case figures as the person running it, errors carry stable codes, and no stack trace or query text reaches people who are not admins. The error list says when it shows only the newest 200.
    • Jobs say what they did. A backfill that a move to the Free plan cut short finishes Failed and says why, Purge now waits while summaries are rebuilt, and the object screen's Backfill card lists the last Catch up.
    • The Elapsed Business Hours and Elapsed Calendar Hours fields replace Business Hours and Calendar Hours on Intervals, so a duration is never confused with a Business hours schedule.
    • Numbers and months follow your locale, every field has a description and help text, and the install guide adds the step that sets each dashboard's running user.

    Sprint 8: Free and Plus plans, hardening, and performance runs (18 October 2026)#

    • Free and Plus plans. Sojourn installs on the Free plan: tracking Case, Lead, and Opportunity, the record timeline, the reports and dashboards, goals and breach flags, business hours, pause and Catch up, summaries, the Health Monitor, and settings export and import. The Plus plan adds tracking for any other object, Live values on the record, Backfill with History or Seed, purge and the archive, and Omni-Channel agent work. On Free, Plus features stay visible in Sojourn Settings with their controls off and an Upgrade to Plus link. Moving an org from Plus to Free deletes nothing. See Free and Plus plans.
    • New guide pages: Check the prerequisites, Track Cases and open your first report, Run the jobs, Troubleshooting, and Uninstall Sojourn. The install guide gains the upgrade steps, and Assign permission sets explains that Intervals are Public Read Only and how to make them Private.
    • Checks on every change: every Sojourn screen method checks a Sojourn permission before it reads or writes, production code has no test-only switches, no record Id is written into code or metadata, and only the ChangeCapture entry point is global.
    • Performance: 2,000 Cases loaded through Bulk API 2.0 with Status and owner tracked cost Sojourn about 400 ms of CPU per 200-record chunk, and summaries, reconcile, and purge were timed on 72,003 Intervals. The results are in Reports at large volume.

    Sprint 7: the record timeline, reports, and dashboards (14 October 2026)#

    • The Sojourn Timeline on record pages and Experience Cloud pages: a lane per Tracked field, each stay sized by its time, goal ticks and the time past a goal, details on focus or hover, and totals by value and by owner. History purged to the archive shows too, marked as archived.
    • The Sojourn Timeline permission, in the Sojourn User, Manager, Health Monitor, and Admin permission sets, and a new Sojourn Site User permission set for Experience Cloud users. The timeline shows the history of a record only to people who can open the record.
    • 20 reports in the Sojourn Reports folder and 4 dashboards in Sojourn Dashboards: Desk Productivity, Process Bottlenecks, SLA Health, and Leaderboards. Trends and leaderboards read the daily summaries, so they stay fast at any volume.
    • The report cookbook gains worked examples, hours per Case, status groups, and reports at large volume, and the reference lists every report and dashboard with the filters it relies on.
    • Live values, goals, and capture use the same status field as the Case metrics when an object has none chosen.

    Sprint 6: Case metrics, Omni-Channel, and breach alerts (13 October 2026)#

    • Case metrics on every Case: first closed at, business minutes to first close, reopens, owner and status changes, and business minutes held by queues, by agents, and in total. They are rebuilt from the Case's own Intervals whoever saves the Case, and by Backfill and repair.
    • The Sojourn Omni-Channel package: one Agent work Interval per agent assignment of routed work, closed with its minutes when the work ends. It installs only in orgs with Omni-Channel; the core package does not need it.
    • The Sojourn Breach Alert Template Flow: save a copy, activate it, and the Interval owner and their manager get one email when an Interval breaches its goal.
    • The Case Status At Close field on Intervals is removed before the first release.
    • The purge runs list shows how many files each run saved, and a stopped run shows what it deleted before it stopped.

    Sprint 5: summaries, retention, goals, and moving settings (12 October 2026)#

    • Summaries each night: closed Intervals counted into daily and monthly rows, kept when Intervals are purged, with the Sojourn Interval Summaries report type and Rebuild summaries for whole months.
    • Retention and purge: choose Keep everything or Keep a number of days. A purge deletes only closed, summarised Intervals older than the window, saves each part to a CSV file on its purge run first, and can copy them to the archive. Purge now runs one straight away; both ask you to type DELETE.
    • Goals per value of a Tracked field, in business minutes, with a warn-at percent.
    • Business hours per queue, so time owned by a queue counts in that queue's hours.
    • Export settings to a file and import it in another org, with a check of what will change before Apply, and Replace what is here to match the file.
    • At most 10 Tracked fields per object, Start timing at the first value for fields that start blank, and an Estimate of the Interval rows and storage your choice adds.
    • The Sojourn app has three tabs: Sojourn Settings, Health Monitor, and Intervals.
    • The Health Monitor shows a Flow you cannot see as information rather than a warning, and a catch up keeps its window when capture was paused during the run.

    Sprint 4: jobs and the Health Monitor (12 October 2026)#

    • Backfill from an object's screen: History builds past Intervals from field history, Seed starts each record from its current values, and Fresh starts with the next change. The card shows Queued, Running, Complete, or Failed with what was done.
    • Catch up after a pause: the records changed while capture was paused get their Intervals at the time they changed, and running it again is safe.
    • Live values: minutes to date and breach flags on open Intervals, and the minute fields on each record, every 10, 15, 20, 30, or 60 minutes.
    • Reconcile each night: flags Intervals of deleted or merged records, repairs drift from the record's current values, and retries Limit and DML errors.
    • A Jobs card on the Sojourn Settings overview to turn Live values and Reconcile on, change their schedule, and see the last and next run.
    • The Health Monitor tab: overall health, capture checks, job runs, error counts, and an error list with Create Interval, Ignore, and Mark resolved, each error with what happened, why, and how to fix it.
    • The Sojourn Health Monitor permission set, so a support or operations group can fix errors without Sojourn Settings.
    • Tracking errors are now the Error Log, with a Create Interval action on each record.
    • The Sojourn Settings screens match the approved designs, and the Tracked objects list shows each object's Tracked fields, Track owner, and where its business hours come from.

    Sprint 3: Flow template and Sojourn Settings (12 October 2026)#

    • The Sojourn Settings page, with a Sojourn menu that folds into icons and remembers the choice.
    • Add any object Sojourn can track from a searchable list, with no API names to type.
    • Choose Tracked fields, Track owner, status field and closed values, name field, business hours, up to five snapshot fields, record types, and Live values fields on one screen.
    • Pause and Resume capture per object, with the time the pause started.
    • A capture status for every Tracked object: Tracked by package, Flow active, Flow missing, or Flow unknown, with Re-check.
    • The Sojourn Capture Template Flow and four steps on screen to turn on capture for any other object.
    • Org-wide Tracking enabled and default business hours, saved with Save settings.
    • Shorter API names: the namespace already names the package, so components no longer repeat it.

    Sprint 2: capture engine (11 October 2026)#

    • Automatic capture on Case, Lead, and Opportunity: a save that changes a Tracked field closes the open Interval and opens the next.
    • The Sojourn: Capture Change Flow action, for any other object.
    • Time with each owner, user or queue, through a Tracked field on the owner.
    • Business and calendar minutes on every Interval, with business hours chosen per field, record, queue, object, or org.
    • Pause Capture and Paused Since on the Tracked object, for bulk imports.
    • The source of every Interval: Trigger, Flow, or Catch-Up.
    • Tracking errors instead of failed saves, of type DML, Limit, Config, BusinessHours, or Unknown.

    Sprint 1: data model (11 October 2026)#

    • Configuration objects: Tracked object and Tracked field, with queue business hours and Goals.
    • The Interval object, which records each stay in a value of a Tracked field, with business and calendar minutes.
    • Interval summaries, tracking errors, purge runs, and the Interval archive big object.
    • Fields Sojourn adds to Case, Lead, and Opportunity.
    • Permission sets Sojourn Admin, Sojourn Manager, and Sojourn User, with tabs and the Sojourn app.
    • Report types for intervals, joins to Case, Lead, and Opportunity, summaries, Goals, and errors.

    Sprint 0: foundation (11 October 2026)#

    • Package structure for Sojourn and Sojourn Omni-Channel.
    • Org-wide settings in the Sojourn Settings custom setting.
    • Continuous integration with Salesforce Code Analyzer and a 90 percent coverage gate.
