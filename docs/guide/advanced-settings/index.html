    Guide

    Tune the advanced settings

    Set how much of each Salesforce limit Sojourn leaves for your own automation and which Omni-Channel statuses start agent work, or put the advanced settings back to their defaults.

    Salesforce admin About 5 minutes Since release 1

    Most orgs never change these. Open Sojourn Settings, then Advanced under Operations. Each setting names its default, and a blank box always means the default, so an org that never opens this screen runs as Sojourn ships. Changing a setting needs the Customize Application permission, as the Overview settings do.

    Limits in a save#

    Sojourn records Intervals inside your users' saves, so it shares each save's Salesforce limits with your own triggers, Flows, and other apps: in a user's save, 100 queries, 10,000 rows written, and 10 seconds of CPU time. Before it does its work, Sojourn checks that the save would still have this much left over for everything else. When it would not, Sojourn steps aside: it writes a Limit error to the Error Log instead of the Interval, the user's save goes through, and the nightly Reconcile retries the change. See Watch Sojourn in the Health Monitor.

    Setting What it leaves for your automation Default Range
    Queries left for your automation Queries the save can still run 10 1 to 50
    Rows left for your automation Rows the save can still insert, update, or delete 2,000 100 to 9,000
    CPU left for your automation, milliseconds CPU time the save can still spend 2,000 500 to 9,000

    A higher number leaves more for your automation, and Sojourn steps aside sooner, so more changes wait for Reconcile; a lower number does the opposite. None of them can be 0, so Sojourn never spends the last of a limit.

    Each box shows the saved number, or the default while none is saved. Clear a box to go back to its default, then choose Save limits. A number outside its range is refused, for example "Queries left for your automation: enter a whole number from 1 to 50, or leave it blank for the default, 10.", and nothing is saved.

    Omni-Channel#

    The Omni-Channel card shows when Omni-Channel is turned on in the org. Agent work starts at lists the Agent Work statuses at which the Sojourn Omni-Channel package opens an Agent work Interval: Assigned and Opened unless you change them. Choose statuses from the org's own list, at least one, and choose Save statuses. Omni-Channel agent work is on the Plus plan, so on the Free plan the card shows the statuses with its controls off and the Plus notice.

    Reset to defaults#

    Reset to defaults puts seven settings back to their defaults: the four on this screen and three on others. The dialog names them as their screens do:

    • Live values: keep current for (days), on the Jobs screen
    • Queries left for your automation
    • Rows left for your automation
    • CPU left for your automation, milliseconds
    • Intervals per purge part, under Retention and purge (see The purge part size)
    • Day rows become Month rows after (days), on Summaries
    • Agent work starts at

    Tracking enabled, the default business hours, the retention period and the archive, Breach alerts, the job schedules, and every Tracked object, Tracked field, and goal are kept.

    1. Choose Reset to defaults. The dialog lists the seven settings.
    2. Type RESET in capitals. Reset to defaults in the dialog turns on only when the word matches.
    3. Choose Reset to defaults. A notice says "Advanced settings are back to their defaults."

    You are done when

    • Each box on the Advanced screen shows the number you saved, or its default, and its hint names the default.
    • After a reset, the Live values window on the Jobs screen reads 90 days again.
