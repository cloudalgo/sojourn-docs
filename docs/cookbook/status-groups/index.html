    Report cookbook

    Status groups

    Edit the Status group bucket so the share-of-time report matches your statuses.

    Share of Time per Status Group sums business minutes per status group: time waiting on the customer against time with your company. The package cannot know your status values, so the report ships with example values in a bucket field named Status group:

    Group Example statuses
    Customer Waiting on Customer
    Company New, Working, Escalated
    Other Every status not listed

    Edit the bucket#

    1. Open Share of Time per Status Group and select Save As into your folder.
    2. In the report builder, open the Status group column's menu and select Edit Bucket Column.
    3. Move each of your statuses into Customer or Company, add groups if you need them, and save.

    A report has at most 5 bucket fields, 20 buckets per field, and 20 values per bucket.
